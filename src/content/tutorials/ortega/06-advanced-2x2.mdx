I have no advanced 2x2 tutorials on this site, but I can point you to some resources elsewhere.

## [Waterman Method](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/index.php/CLL)

In a nutshell:

1. Solve one layer.
2. Use one of 42 [CLL algorithms](http://algdb.net/puzzle/222/cll) to orient and permute the last layer simultaneously.

Two of the 42 CLL algorithms are PLLs.

## [Erik-Gunnar (EG) Method](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/index.php/EG_Method)

In a nutshell:

1. Solve one side. (This is the same first step as the Ortega/Varasano method.)
2. Use one of 128 algorithms to orient the opposite side and permute the entire cube.

Roughly one-third of these algorithms are the 42 [CLL algorithms](http://algdb.net/puzzle/222/cll) used in the Waterman method. The other algorithms peform CLL on the last layer while swapping two pieces on the bottom layer, either adjacent ([EG 1](http://algdb.net/puzzle/222/eg1)) or opposite ([EG 2](http://algdb.net/puzzle/222/eg2)) corners.
