PLL permutes the last layer corners and edges in one algorithm. There is nothing intuitive about PLL. You recognize which case you have, and you execute the algorithm for it as fluidly and quickly as you can. Sound familiar?

2-look PLL has only 6 algorithms. I have [flashcards](../../../flashcards/?set=pll2) for them.

Full PLL has 21 cases. I have [flashcards](../../../flashcards/?set=pll) for all them.

## Tips

The tips I gave for [OLL](../orient-last-layer/) also apply to PLL. Learn to recognize the case immediately, but AUF if needed.

There are many possible algorithms for each case, so please explore to find the ones you like best. There are good lists of them [here](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/index.php/PLL) and [here](http://algdb.net/puzzle/333/pll).

Once you become familiar with CFOP, your journey is far from over. There are many [supplements, variants, and alternate methods](../beyond-cfop/) which await.
