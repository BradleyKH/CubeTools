I am not a Roux expert, but I can point you to those who are.

[Kian Mansour](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/persons/2015MANS03) has achieved sub-7 averages with the Roux method. His [homepage](https://sites.google.com/view/kianroux) and [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCdEm9-DsuOzNoWmTAYNLRoQ) have great resources to help you progress in Roux.

Some advanced concepts in Roux include:

- Build the blocks in a different order. Sometimes the center is last!
- Influencing the second block while building the first.
- Build the two blocks simultaneously.
- EOLR: solve edge orientation while setting up UL/UR. Kian has a nice [tutorial](https://sites.google.com/view/kianroux/eolr) on this, and [Gilles Roux's tutorial](http://grrroux.free.fr/method/Step_4.html) also deals with it.

Happy cubing!
